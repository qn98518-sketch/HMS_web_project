Metric definitions and sources
  • Appointments: number of scheduled appointments in each month, across all statuses.
  • Revenue by bill month: current paid amounts on non-cancelled bills, grouped by bill date. This is not cash received during that month. Amounts use the same currency as Bills.
  • New patients: patient profiles first created in each month, counted once in their creation month.
  • Sources: Appointments, Bills and Patients. Existing records are counted regardless of current account role, lock status or soft deletion.
  • Comparisons use the same months of the previous year and are omitted for incomplete or future periods. A dash means unavailable data; zero means no matching records.
  • The current month is in progress. Future scheduled appointments can appear; future revenue and new-patient months are left blank.
